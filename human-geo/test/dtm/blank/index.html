Draw the five stages of the demographic transition and fill in every label, word for word.

Blanks

Like the test gives you the axes, the stage headings and the row labels, as your teacher's test does. Draw the three lines with your finger, stylus or mouse (give each line its own color from the toolbar at the bottom). Then label them: tap Label, tap the chart where a label goes and type it (drag the dots on its left to move it). Fill in every table cell word for word, and draw each stage's pyramid in its box. Warm-up draws the lines for you; Everything blank adds every label and the title. Press Check when you're done.
